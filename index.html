How to Play ×

Bracket Battle is a pick-em competition built around the NCAA Division I Men's Lacrosse Championship. Before a single game is played, you pick the winner of every game across all four rounds — all 15 of them. Then you lock in, watch the games, and see how your bracket holds up.

Scoring
Round Correct Pick
First Round+10 pts
Quarterfinals+15 pts
Semifinals+20 pts
Championship+25 pts
Tiebreaker

If two players finish with the same score, the player with more correctly called upsets wins. An upset is when a lower-seeded or unseeded team beats a seeded opponent.

The Bracket

Each card shows the real matchup. The blue bar on a team row means that's who you picked. After the deadline your bracket is locked — no changes. Results and scores update automatically as games are played.
