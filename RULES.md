# Bracket Battle — Player Rules

**Play at:** [ehall4444.github.io/bracket-battle](https://ehall4444.github.io/bracket-battle)

---

## What Is Bracket Battle?

Bracket Battle is a friendly competition built around the NCAA Division I Men's Lacrosse Championship. Sixteen teams enter. You pick the winner of every game — all 15 of them — before a single whistle blows. Then you sit back, watch the games, and see how well your bracket holds up against reality.

---

## How It Works

The tournament runs over three weekends across four rounds:

- **First Round** — 8 games, 16 teams
- **Quarterfinals** — 4 games, 8 teams
- **Semifinals** — 2 games, 4 teams
- **Championship** — 1 game, 2 teams

You make all your picks upfront — across all four rounds — before the First Round begins. Once the deadline passes, your bracket is locked. No changes.

---

## Making Your Picks

1. Register for an account and sign in
2. Start with the **First Round** tab and pick a winner for each of the 8 games
3. Once all 8 First Round picks are made, the **Quarterfinals** tab unlocks
4. Work through Quarterfinals, Semifinals, and the Championship the same way
5. Hit **Save My Bracket** when all 15 picks are complete

You must complete and save all 15 picks before the deadline. Partial brackets cannot be saved.

---

## The Deadline

The deadline is posted on the bracket page and counts down in real time. Once it passes:

- All brackets are locked permanently
- No new registrations or saves are accepted
- The fun begins

---

## Scoring

Points are awarded for correct picks as each game result is confirmed:

| Round | Correct Pick |
|---|---|
| First Round | +10 pts |
| Quarterfinals | +15 pts |
| Semifinals | +20 pts |
| Championship | +25 pts |

Wrong picks score nothing. Only correct picks move you up the leaderboard.

---

## Tiebreakers

If two players finish with the same score:

1. **Most upset picks correctly called** — an upset is when a lower-seeded or unseeded team wins
2. If still tied, it's a tie

---

## Following Along

Once the deadline passes, navigate freely between all four round tabs to see:

- The real matchups as they happen
- Your picks highlighted against the actual teams playing
- Which of your picks advanced and which didn't
- Your running score updated as results come in

Check the **Leaderboard** at any time to see where you stand against the group.
